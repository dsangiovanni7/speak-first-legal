Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manifesta ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use our iOS application. We built Manifesta to work almost entirely on your device, and this policy reflects that design choice.

1. Summary

  • Manifesta does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Your affirmations, streaks, settings, and app-blocking preferences are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • If the App uses your microphone (for example, to let you record or speak affirmations), audio is processed on your device in the moment and is not recorded to a file, uploaded, or stored by us.
  • Manifesta uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to let you block or limit other apps. Apple designs this framework so that Manifesta never sees which specific apps you have selected or how you use them — see Section 4 for detail.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Manifesta does not use accounts, cloud sync, or a backend server for your personal content, we do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials
  • Recordings of your voice or audio you speak into the App
  • The specific apps you choose to block, or your usage patterns within them
  • Your affirmations, streak history, or in-app content

3. Microphone Use

Manifesta may request access to your device's microphone to support features such as voice-guided affirmations. When the microphone is used:

  • Audio is processed live, on your device, only to power the feature you are actively using (for example, detecting that you spoke, or providing voice playback/interaction).
  • We do not save audio recordings, transmit audio to our servers, or share audio with third parties.
  • You can revoke microphone access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Manifesta on your iPhone. Doing so will only disable microphone-dependent features; the rest of the App will continue to work.

4. Screen Time & App Blocking (Family Controls)

Manifesta uses Apple's Family Controls, Screen Time, DeviceActivity, and Managed Settings frameworks to let you restrict or block distracting apps as part of your self-improvement routine. Apple designs these frameworks with privacy protections built directly into the operating system:

  • When you choose which apps or categories to restrict using Apple's app-selection screen, Apple's system "uses opaque values to represent the selections" so that, in Apple's own words, you can "specify applications, web domains, and categories without revealing their choices to the app." In practice, this means Manifesta never learns the actual identity of the apps you've chosen to block — only an opaque, system-generated token that Manifesta uses to apply and enforce the restriction you configured.
  • Manifesta does not receive a list of the other apps installed on your device.
  • Manifesta does not track how much time you spend in other apps beyond enforcing the limits you configure.
  • All of this configuration is stored locally on your device, managed by Apple's system frameworks — not on our servers.
  • Because Apple treats access to these frameworks as a sensitive capability, Manifesta's use of them was individually reviewed and approved by Apple before release.

5. Information Handled by Third Parties

Manifesta uses the following third-party services to operate core functionality. Both act as data processors on our behalf, and both are contractually and technically required to protect your information to a standard equal to or greater than what is described in this Privacy Policy:

  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and entitlement status. RevenueCat receives a device type, a purchase/receipt token, and transaction data from Apple (such as purchase and renewal events) needed to validate your subscription, along with an app-generated identifier. RevenueCat acts solely as a data processor, not a controller, of this information. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email unless you separately provide one. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for detail.
  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) — processes your subscription payment directly. Manifesta never sees or stores your payment card details; Apple handles billing under its own privacy policy.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising purposes.

6. Data Stored On Your Device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your affirmations and any custom content you create
  • Your streak and progress history
  • App preferences and settings, including your Screen Time / app-blocking configuration

If you delete Manifesta from your device, this locally stored data is removed along with it (subject to anything retained in an iCloud device backup you control through Apple).

7. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Similar Laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We honor the spirit of these rights as follows:

  • Access, correction, deletion, and portability: Because Manifesta does not hold your personal content (affirmations, streaks, settings) on any server, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or export on your behalf — this data lives only on your device, under your direct control. You can view, change, or permanently remove it at any time from within the App, or remove all of it at once by deleting the App.
  • Data we do process as a processor's controller (the limited purchase/subscription data described in Section 5) can be corrected or deleted by contacting us using the information in Section 10, or by contacting Apple directly, since Apple originates that data.
  • California residents: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and based on our size and the minimal data we handle, we do not believe the CCPA/CPRA's disclosure thresholds apply to us. We are including this section as a good-faith disclosure regardless.
  • To exercise any of these rights or ask a question about them, contact us using the information in Section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

Manifesta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently received personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.

9. Your Choices

  • You can disable microphone access at any time in your device Settings.
  • You can remove your Screen Time / app-blocking configuration at any time from within the App or from iOS Settings → Screen Time.
  • You can delete the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App.
