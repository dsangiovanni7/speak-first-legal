Common questions

How do I unlock an app?

Open a locked app and tap "Speak first". Open Speak First from the notification, say your affirmation out loud, then tap "Unlock app". Your app opens for the time you chose and locks again automatically. If you need an app without speaking, tap "Take a pass" (3 per month).

What are Morning Lock and Night Lock?

They are optional stricter locks. Morning Lock protects the hour after you wake up. Night Lock starts one hour before your bedtime and lasts until you wake up. By default Morning Lock applies Monday to Friday and Night Lock applies on nights before a weekday (Sunday to Thursday), and you can choose your own days. During these windows an affirmation does not unlock your apps. If you truly need one, use Emergency unlock: say an affirmation to unlock for 30 minutes. You get 2 emergency unlocks per week, and they reset every Monday.

Why can't I change my settings right away?

Changes that make your protection less strict, such as removing a day or an app, turning blocking off, or lengthening the unlock time, take effect after 24 hours. Changes that make your protection stricter apply right away.

Speak First is not locking my apps.

Make sure Screen Time access is on for Speak First in Settings, and that you have selected the apps to protect.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Speak First. You can also use Manage Subscription inside the app.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchase.
