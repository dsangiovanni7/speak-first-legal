Common questions

How do I unlock an app?

Open a locked app and say your affirmation out loud. Speak First listens, and your app opens for the time you chose.

Speak First is not locking my apps.

Make sure Screen Time access is on for Speak First in Settings, and that you have selected the apps to protect.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Speak First. You can also use Manage Subscription inside the app.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchase.
